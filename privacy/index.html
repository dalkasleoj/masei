Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Masei has no account, no server, and no analytics. The information it works with stays on your device.

What it uses

The city you are in, from Location Services. The photos you choose to keep with a day, read from your photo library (read-only). The notes you write. Together, this is your journal.

Where it lives

On your device, in the app's own storage, and in any backup of your device that you make with iCloud or your computer. Masei does not send it anywhere else.

What it never does

It does not sell or share your data, use it for tracking or advertising, or include third-party analytics or advertising code.

Looking up places

To turn coordinates into a city name, and to search for a place you type in, Masei uses Apple's Maps services. Apple receives the coordinates or search text for those lookups under Apple's privacy policy. Masei does not send them to anyone else.

Your control

You can turn off Location or Photos for Masei at any time in Settings. You can delete any day, or all of your data, inside the app, or by deleting the app.

Children

Masei is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
