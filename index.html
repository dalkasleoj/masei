Masei

A private journal of where you have been.

Masei (מַסְעֵי) is Hebrew for "journeys of".

Masei quietly notes the city you are in each day and turns it into a journal of days, a globe of everywhere you have been, trips it finds on its own, and numbers for the year. There is no account and no server: your log stays on your device.

Common questions

Why does Masei ask for "Always" location?
Only so it can note your city once a day while the app is closed. It never tracks a route, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Where is my data?
On your device, in the app's own storage, and in a Masei folder in your own iCloud Drive if you use iCloud Drive. Masei has no server of its own to hold it.

How do I delete my data?
Delete any day inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything. You can also turn off Location or Photos for Masei in Settings at any time.

How do I move to a new phone?
Restore from your device backup, or open Masei, go to Settings → Backup → iCloud Drive and tap Restore. Your journal and its weekly backups are in the Masei folder in iCloud Drive.
